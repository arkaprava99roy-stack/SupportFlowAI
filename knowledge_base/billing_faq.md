# SupportFlow Billing & Payment FAQ

## 1. Accepted Payment Methods
We support the following secure payment methods:
- Credit & Debit Cards: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, JCB.
- Digital Wallets: Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay.
- PayPal: Standard checkout and PayPal Pay in 4 installment plans.
- Wire Transfers: Accepted only for enterprise bulk orders over $5,000.

## 2. Invoices & Receipts
- Detailed digital VAT/GST-compliant invoices are automatically emailed upon order confirmation.
- You can also view and download PDF receipts anytime from your user dashboard under **Account > Billing & Order History**.

## 3. Recurring Subscriptions & Autopay
- Subscriptions automatically renew on the billing date shown in your dashboard (monthly or annually).
- You may cancel subscription renewals at any time up to 24 hours prior to the next renewal date without cancellation penalties.
- If cancelled, you retain access to the premium tier until the conclusion of the active billing cycle.

## 4. Failed Charges & Grace Period
- If a scheduled payment fails due to insufficient funds, card expiration, or bank decline, our system retries every 48 hours for up to 3 attempts.
- A **5-day grace period** is granted before account services or premium features are temporarily suspended.
- Update your billing payment method promptly under **Settings > Payment Details** to avoid interruption.

## 5. Duplicate or Unauthorized Charges
- If you notice a duplicate charge on your bank statement, please verify whether it is a temporary pre-authorization hold.
- If a duplicate charge is confirmed posted, contact billing support immediately with the transaction date, card last 4 digits, and order confirmation numbers.
- Any legitimate duplicate charge will be refunded within 24 to 48 hours.
