# SupportFlow Shipping & Delivery Policy

## 1. Shipping Methods & Delivery Windows
We provide multiple delivery tiers depending on destination:
- **Standard Ground Shipping**: 3 to 5 business days (Free for domestic orders over $50).
- **Expedited Express Shipping**: 2 business days (Guaranteed delivery by 8 PM).
- **Overnight Next-Day Delivery**: 1 business day for orders placed before 1:00 PM EST.
- **International Priority Shipping**: 7 to 14 business days depending on customs clearance.

## 2. Order Processing & Dispatch
- Orders are processed and dispatched from our fulfillment center Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays.
- Standard orders placed before 2:00 PM EST are dispatched the same day; orders placed after 2:00 PM EST ship the next business day.
- A shipping confirmation email containing your Carrier (FedEx, UPS, DHL, USPS) and live Tracking Number is dispatched immediately upon shipment.

## 3. Tracking Your Package
- Track your package in real-time by visiting **Orders > Track Package** in your account dashboard.
- Tracking numbers may take 12 to 24 hours to update in the carrier system after label creation.

## 4. Lost, Delayed, or Damaged Packages
- If tracking shows no movement for more than 5 consecutive business days, contact our shipping desk.
- If your package is marked as "Delivered" but cannot be found:
  1. Check around porches, side doors, garages, and with neighbors.
  2. Wait 24 hours (carriers occasionally scan packages prematurely).
  3. If still missing, file an inquiry with our support team within 10 days of delivery status.
- For items damaged in transit, photograph the outer box and damaged goods and reach out for an instant replacement.
