# SupportFlow Refund & Return Policy

## 1. 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee
Customers are eligible for a full refund on standard items within **30 days** of delivery. To qualify for a full refund:
- The item must be in its original packaging with all tags, accessories, and documentation included.
- The item must be unused, unwashed, and in undamaged, resalable condition.
- Proof of purchase (Order ID or invoice number) must be provided.

## 2. Non-Refundable Items & Exceptions
The following items and services cannot be refunded or returned:
- Digital downloads, software licenses, and gift cards once redeemed or delivered.
- Personalized, engraved, or customized merchandise.
- Perishable goods, intimacy products, and health/personal hygiene products if unsealed.
- Final clearance items marked explicitly as "Non-Returnable".

## 3. Refund Processing Timelines
- Once your returned item arrives at our fulfillment center, inspection takes **2 to 3 business days**.
- Upon inspection approval, the refund is issued to your original payment method:
  - **Credit / Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)**: 3 to 5 business days depending on your issuing bank.
  - **PayPal**: 1 to 2 business days.
  - **Store Credit / Digital Wallet**: Instant upon approval.
- Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (e.g., defective or incorrect item sent).

## 4. Defective or Damaged Products
If you receive a defective, damaged, or incorrect item:
- Contact SupportFlow support within **7 days** of package delivery.
- Provide clear photos or a short video of the defect along with your Order ID.
- We will issue a prepaid return shipping label and ship an expedited replacement at no additional charge or issue a 100% full refund including shipping.

## 5. Late or Missing Refunds
If you have not received your refund after 7 business days from the approval confirmation:
1. Check your bank account or credit card statement again.
2. Contact your bank or credit card company, as processing times vary.
3. If you still have not received your refund, contact our billing team at `billing@supportflow.ai` with your Order ID and RMA confirmation.
