# SupportFlow Account Management & Profile Help

## 1. Password Reset & Account Recovery
- If you forgot your password, click **"Forgot Password?"** on the login page.
- An automated password reset link will be sent to your registered email address within 2 minutes. The link is valid for **30 minutes**.
- If you do not receive the email, verify your spam/junk folder or verify that you used the correct registered address.

## 2. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
- We strongly recommend enabling Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for heightened account security.
- To enable 2FA: Navigate to **Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication**.
- We support TOTP authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password) and SMS verification codes.
- Store your one-time backup recovery codes safely in a secure password manager.

## 3. Updating Profile, Email, and Billing Addresses
- You can update your full name, phone number, and primary shipping address directly in **Account Settings > Profile**.
- For security reasons, updating your primary email address requires confirmation from both the old and new email addresses.

## 4. Account Deletion & Data Privacy (GDPR/CCPA)
- Under GDPR and CCPA regulations, you have the right to request full erasure of your personal data.
- To request account deletion, navigate to **Settings > Privacy & Data > Delete Account**.
- Once submitted, your account enters a **14-day cooling-off period** before all personal data, order histories, and payment tokens are permanently purged.
